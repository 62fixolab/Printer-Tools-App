## Features
Explore the advanced features that will make your experience unique.

#### Monitoring
Monitor your prints in real time with remaining layers, remaining time, and nozzle, chamber and bed temperatures.

#### Cameras
Watch your prints live, with support for external cameras over **HTTP, RTSP, RTSPS, TCP (JPEG) and WebRTC**.

#### Multiple printers
Manage 3D printers from different brands in a single app, ideal for multiple printers or full print farms.

#### Themeable
Choose your accent color and personalize your dashboard to suit your style.

#### Light & Dark
All components support both light and dark color modes.

#### Responsive
Adaptable components that look amazing on **iOS, iPadOS, macOS and watchOS**, and on **Android phones, tablets and Wear OS**.

#### Local by design
Printer Tools talks to your printers over your own network. No manufacturer cloud account is required.

### Remote access

- **OctoEverywhere** for Bambu Lab, Klipper/Moonraker, OctoPrint and PrusaLink printers
- **Obico** (cloud or self-hosted) for OctoPrint and Klipper/Moonraker
- Or a VPN back to your home network for any printer

Full details per brand at [printertools.app/compatibility](https://printertools.app/compatibility).

## Add-on Support

Extend your setup with the hardware you already own.

- **BIGTREETECH**: Panda PWR, Panda Touch and Panda Knomi
- **WLED**: turn LED strips on or off and adjust brightness
- **Sinilink**: live temperature and relay status of bed and chamber heaters
- **External Cameras**: ONVIF discovery, or any IP camera over HTTP, RTSP, RTSPS, TCP (JPEG) or WebRTC
- **Spoolman**: track filament spools and set the active spool on Klipper
- **multiACE**: monitor and control every ACE unit on the Snapmaker U1, including drying and toolhead assignment

More at [printertools.app/addons](https://printertools.app/addons).

## More features
Exceeding your expectations with a wide range of powerful features.

#### File manager
Manage and organize all your files in one place. View detailed information, sort them and send them to print.

#### GCODE & 3MF inspector
Check layers, estimated time, required material and print configuration before sending a file to the printer.

#### View and export
Share your prints with images, videos or timelapses through your favorite messaging app, or save them to your photo library.

#### Widgets & Live Activities
Follow print progress from the Home Screen, the Lock Screen, the Dynamic Island and your Apple Watch.
