# Printer Tools App

The definitive dashboard for your 3D printer. Connect printers from BambuLab, Klipper, OctoPrint and Creality from anywhere.

[![Printer Tools App Screenshot](https://printertools.app/assets/home/en/three-phones.png)](https://printertools.app)

---

## Features  
Explore the advanced features that will make your experience unique.

#### Monitoring  
Monitor your prints in real time with detailed information such as remaining layers, estimated time, nozzle, chamber, and bed temperatures.

#### Cameras  
View your prints live with support for external cameras using **HTTP, RTSP, and RTSPS** protocols.

#### Multiple printers  
Manage 3D printers from different brands in a single app — ideal for users with multiple printers or full print farms.

#### Themeable  
Choose your accent color and personalize your dashboard to match your workflow and style.

#### Light & Dark  
All components support both light and dark color modes.

#### Responsive  
Adaptable components that look amazing on **iOS, iPadOS, macOS, and watchOS**.

## More features  
Exceeding your expectations with a wide range of powerful tools.

#### Gallery  
Browse your completed prints with images, videos, and timelapses in one unified gallery.

#### File manager  
Organize and manage all your files in one place. View detailed file information, sort them easily, and send files directly to print.

#### GCODE & 3MF inspector  
Inspect files before printing with detailed data about layers, estimated print time, required material, and print configuration.

#### View and export  
Share your prints effortlessly via your favorite messaging apps or export images, videos, and timelapses directly to your photo library.
